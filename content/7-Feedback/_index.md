### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is open and supportive. The FCAJ team members are always willing to help when I encounter difficulties, and the learning atmosphere encourages questions and experimentation. The workspace and documentation are well organized, which helped me stay focused. For future cohorts, adding more informal team bonding activities could make collaboration even stronger.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor provided clear, step-by-step guidance, especially for the AWS setup and workshop tasks. I appreciated that I was encouraged to try solutions independently first, then receive feedback to improve. The admin team also supported logistics, documentation access, and made the internship experience smooth.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The assigned tasks matched well with my academic background in cloud computing and software development, while also exposing me to practical AWS topics that I had not practiced before. This balance helped me reinforce theory and gain hands-on experience.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
I learned valuable skills in cloud infrastructure, website building with Hugo, cost monitoring, security configuration, and documentation. The program also helped me improve my ability to follow a structured learning path and turn workshop content into real deliverables.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The culture is positive and respectful. Everyone treats each other professionally, and the team is willing to cooperate when there are urgent tasks. This environment made me feel included and motivated.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program offers useful support such as an internship allowance and flexible scheduling when needed. Access to internal training and workshop materials was especially helpful.
